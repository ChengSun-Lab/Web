Professor Cheng Sun participates in Phoenix TV program exploring science and human well-being

November 2026 · Science communication & outreach

Prof. Sun joined a public dialogue on science, technology, and human life through the Phoenix TV program The Happiness of Carbon-Based Life.

Professor Cheng Sun from the University of Science and Technology of China recently participated in The Happiness of Carbon-Based Life (《碳基的幸福生活》), a Phoenix TV program exploring the relationship between science, technology, and human life.

The program brings together perspectives from different disciplines to encourage public discussion about scientific progress, emerging technologies, and their implications for society.

As a researcher in cancer immunology and spatial biology, Professor Sun's scientific work focuses on understanding the complex interactions between tumors and the immune system, with the goal of advancing precision cancer medicine.

His participation reflects a broader commitment to scientific communication and interdisciplinary dialogue, extending beyond specialized research to engage with questions about technology, human well-being, and the future of science.

The Sun Lab values opportunities to connect scientific research with wider public conversations and to promote greater understanding of how fundamental discoveries may contribute to society.
